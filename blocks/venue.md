# Venue

Join us!

All the lectures are to be held in Polo Porta Nuova’s PN10 classroom. You can find the main entrance of the pole in via Nelli if you arrive walking or by train (at 500m there is [Pisa S. Rossore train station](https://goo.gl/maps/zY8G7nZtULx)), but you can enter as well from the secondary entrance [here](https://goo.gl/maps/Lrrtu7RBSX22), near the parking area in via Leonardo da Vinci and via Padre Fedi. Lots of free parking in via Tino di Camaino, [here](https://goo.gl/maps/4FwWJZ9hFtq).
