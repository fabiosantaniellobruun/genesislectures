# Footer boxes

VENUE

Genesis Lectures will be held in spring 2019 for their 4th edition at Polo Porta Nuova PN10, in via Marinello Nelli, Pisa. The conference room can host up to 120 people, but it gets full early: remember to get there early enough.

[GET DIRECTIONS](https://goo.gl/maps/by2hi3P2Pkr)

ORGANIZERS

Genesis was conceived by the Organizing Committee with the support of the Scientific Committee, formed by teachers and students from the Pisa University’s Building Engineering and Architecture MD course.

[ABOUT US](https://www.genesislectures.com/about/)

2016

The very first edition of Genesis Lectures was held in three locations for four conferences: Polo Carmignani, Cinema Nuovo and Piagge Polo’s Aula Magna hosted [Tony Fretton](https://www.genesislectures.com/2016/tony-fretton/), [Guillermo Vázquez Consuegra](https://www.genesislectures.com/2016/guillermo-vazquez-consuegra/), [Ricardo Bak Gordon](https://www.genesislectures.com/2016/ricardo-bak-gordon/) and [Dietmar Eberle](https://www.genesislectures.com/2016/dietmar-eberle/).

[SEE 2016’S GUESTS](https://www.genesislectures.com/2016)

2017

Thanks to the great reception of the public, in 2017 we moved all the lectures in the great Aula Magna of Polo Carmignani, in the very hearth of Pisa’s city center. This year we had the pleasure to listen to [Sou Fujimoto](https://www.genesislectures.com/2017/sou-fujimoto/), [TAMassociati](https://www.genesislectures.com/2017/tamassociati/) and [Elisa Valero Ramos](https://www.genesislectures.com/2017/elisa-valero-ramos/).

[SEE 2017’S GUESTS](https://www.genesislectures.com/2017)

2018

Again in the Aula Magna of Polo Carmignani, the third edition continue its precedessors’ success, reaching nearly five hundreds participats and thousand of views on streaming and YouTube videos. This year joined us [Durish + Nolli](https://www.genesislectures.com/2018/durisch-nolli/), [Robbrecht en Daem](https://www.genesislectures.com/2018/robbrecht-en-daem/) and [Carmody Groarke](https://www.genesislectures.com/2018/carmody-groarke/).
